## Spectral Analysis (9-Channel)

Ray Modeler's "Spectral Analysis (9-Channel)" recipe is an independent
implementation of the published multi-channel (N-channel) spectral simulation
method for Radiance. The scientific method, and in particular the 9-band
wavelength partitioning of the visible spectrum, was developed and published by
the Lark Spectral Lighting project:

- **Lark Spectral Lighting** — https://github.com/larkspectral/Lark_Spectral_Lighting
- Mehlika Inanici, Ph.D. — University of Washington
- ZGF Architects LLP
- Clotilde Pierson, Ph.D. — EPFL / Oregon State University
- Myrta Gkaintatzi-Masouti, M.Sc. — Eindhoven University of Technology
- Bo Jung, M.Sc. and Zining Cheng — University of Washington

Lark Spectral Lighting is distributed under a Modified 3-Clause BSD License.
Ray Modeler contains **no source code** from Lark Spectral Lighting. Its
simulation scripts, binning code and post-processing code are its own. Lark is
credited here as the published origin of the method.

"Lark" and "Lark Spectral Lighting" are names belonging to that project. Ray
Modeler does not use those names for its own features.
